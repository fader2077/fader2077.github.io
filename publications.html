Deep Learning-Based Multi-Model Comparison and Optimization for Ocular Disease Image Classification

Authors: Jie-Xin Li, Syuan-Hao Li* (Corresponding), Po Sen Tsou
Affiliation: Dept. of Computer Science & Information Engineering, National Taitung University, Taiwan
Venue: Journal of Information and Computing (JIC), 2025, 3(2), 14–22
DOI: 10.30211/JIC.202503.007
Submitted: Apr. 07, 2025  ·  Accepted: Jun. 09, 2025

Abstract

Early diagnosis of ocular diseases is critical to prevent vision impairment, yet traditional methods rely heavily on specialists' expertise and face resource allocation challenges. This study systematically evaluates the performance of diverse deep learning architectures (including CNNs, Transformers, and lightweight models) for multi-class ocular disease classification. Utilizing public ocular image datasets with transfer learning and data augmentation to address class imbalance, our experiments demonstrate that self-attention-based models achieve superior accuracy for complex conditions (e.g., diabetic retinopathy), while lightweight architectures significantly improve computational efficiency. The findings provide empirical guidelines for clinical decision-support systems and propose optimization strategies for architecture design, contributing to practical AI-driven healthcare solutions.

Keywords

Deep Learning Data Augmentation Transfer Learning Ocular Disease Detection EfficientNet MobileNet

基於深度學習的眼球病變影像分類：多模型比較與優化

Authors: 李捷新、李軒豪、吳信德、張耀中、賴盈勳
Affiliation: 國立臺東大學 資訊工程學系
Paper ID: IS01-8
Year: 2025

摘要

眼球病變的早期診斷對預防不可逆視力損害至關重要，然而傳統診斷方式高度依賴專業醫師的經驗，且面臨醫療資源分配不均的問題。本研究針對 10 類眼球病變影像資料（如糖尿病視網膜病變、青光眼、視網膜脫離等），系統性比較五種深度學習模型（EfficientNet-B7、MobileNetV3、DenseNet121、GhostNet 及 EVA-02）的分類性能。實驗採用公開數據集（Kaggle Eye Disease Dataset），並透過數據增強與遷移學習解決類別不平衡問題。結果顯示 EfficientNet-B7 在整體準確率（87.16%）和 F1 分數（0.871）上表現最佳，而 MobileNetV3 在計算效率（訓練時間減少 42%）與輕量化方面更具優勢。本成果可作為臨床輔助診斷工具的開發基礎，並提供模型實證依據。

關鍵詞

ZELPHA: A Zoom-Equivariant Lipschitz Prototype Framework for Remote Sensing Aerial Scene Classification

Authors: Hsin-Hung Cho, Syuan-Hao Li, Jie-Xin Li, Hsin-Te Wu
Venue: IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing (J-STARS)
DOI: 10.1109/JSTARS.2026.3669661
Index: IEEE · SCIE
Year: 2025

Abstract

This study introduces ZELPHA, a Zoom-Equivariant Lipschitz Prototype Framework developed to improve robustness in aerial scene classification when image scale varies. The framework combines scale-equivariant feature extraction with Lipschitz-constrained representations to control feature deformation caused by geometric scaling. A prototype-based classification strategy with margin regularization preserves class separability across different scales. Experiments on the UC Merced Land Use dataset show that ZELPHA consistently improves robustness, yielding an average gain of approximately 7.94 percentage points in robust accuracy.

Keywords

Remote Sensing Scale Robustness Lipschitz Stability Zoom-Equivariant Aerial Scene Classification Prototype Learning

Weakly Supervised Watermark Removal Using Multi-View Visual Models and Inpainting

Authors: Yi-Jui Huang, Chia-Cheng Liu, Syuan-Hao Li, Jie-Xin Li
Affiliation (4): Institute of Smart Industry and Green Energy, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University
Venue: 2025 IET International Conference on Engineering Technologies and Applications (IET-ICETA)
Year: 2025

Abstract

This study proposes an integrated approach that combines weakly supervised learning with advanced image inpainting techniques, aiming to achieve precise watermark detection and removal without relying on complex mask annotations. By applying diverse preprocessing strategies, the original images are transformed into multiple visual representations and processed through deep learning models. An ensemble voting mechanism enhances the stability and accuracy of watermark detection. Multi-model feature response maps are fused to determine restoration areas, which are then processed by an inpainting model for masking and visual reconstruction.

Keywords

Abstract

This study applies various data analysis and machine learning techniques to examine electricity consumption data from the steel industry, exploring relationships between different load types and key features. A Multi-Task Deep Learning Framework for Medical Image Analysis Based on Global and Local Feature Extraction

Authors: Jie-Xin Li, Syuan-Hao Li, Bo-Sen Tsou
Venue: ITIPTW 2025
Year: 2025

Abstract

本研究提出一套名為 VovUnet_Var 的全新多任務學習框架，結合 MobileViT 模組與 U-Net 架構，適用於同時進行醫學影像分割與分類任務。研究採用 MedMNIST+ 資料集（解析度 224×224），整合 VoVNet 的 One-Shot Aggregation (OSA) 區塊，設計具備 DownOsa 與 UpOsa 的下/上採樣模組，有效強化多尺度特徵提取能力。MobileViT 融合 CNN 的區域特徵擷取與 Transformer 的全域建模優勢，使框架在多數 MedMNIST+ 子資料集上均優於傳統基準模型，在分類準確率與分割指標上展現穩定且具競爭力的表現。未來將進一步探索參數剪枝（pruning）、知識蒸餾（knowledge distillation）等輕量化策略，並針對 RetinaMNIST 等挑戰性子集導入資料特化前處理，以提升模型在邊緣裝置與低資源環境下的部署能力。

Keywords

Multi-Task Learning MobileViT VoVNet U-Net Medical Image Analysis MedMNIST+ Image Segmentation Image Classification
